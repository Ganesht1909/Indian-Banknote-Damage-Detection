# RBI Guidance Used by the Demo

This repository does not make exchange/refund decisions.

Current RBI public guidance states that bank branches provide facilities for exchange of soiled, mutilated and imperfect notes, subject to the applicable Note Refund Rules and procedures.

The application should therefore use cautious messages such as:
- "Bank branch assessment recommended."
- "Exchange may be available under RBI rules."
- "This model does not determine refund value."

Do not use model confidence as a legal or financial entitlement score.

Official sources:
- RBI Master Direction — Facility for Exchange of Notes and Coins
- Reserve Bank of India (Note Refund) Rules, 2009, as amended
